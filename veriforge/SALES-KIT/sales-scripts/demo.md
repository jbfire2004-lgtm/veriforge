# Demo Script

**Duration:** 45–60 minutes  
**Environment:** `/veriforge/dashboard` and selected engines · forged-metal identity on  
**Rule:** Max **three** engines deep — plus dashboard open/close.

## Setup (before call)

- [ ] `vf-debug*` off  
- [ ] Tenant/demo data loaded  
- [ ] One-pagers for those engines ready to send after  
- [ ] Industry brief matched  

## Act 1 — Identity (5 min)

**Route:** `/veriforge/dashboard` or `/veriforge/homepage`

> This is VeriForge — iron black, steel edges, forge-red for what matters. Safety built like machinery. Angular workflows. Metallic precision.

Point out: Orbitron titles, red underline, metallic KPI headers, steel borders, **no soft corners**.

## Act 2 — Problem mirror (3 min)

> You told us [fragmentation pain]. Watch how one forge replaces swivel-chair tools.

## Act 3 — Engine path (25–35 min)

Pick a path:

### Path A — Compliance-led
1. `/veriforge/compliance` — critical accent, expiry  
2. `/veriforge/training` — readiness link  
3. `/veriforge/command-center` — leadership view  

### Path B — Operations-led
1. `/veriforge/field-operations` or mobile  
2. `/veriforge/incidents` — capture → investigate  
3. `/veriforge/command-center` — escalate  

### Path C — Risk-led
1. `/veriforge/risk`  
2. `/veriforge/predictive`  
3. `/veriforge/digital-twin` — simulate  
4. Brief command-center close  

**Narrate meta:** tenant-aware, auditable, industrial — not a consumer app.

## Act 4 — Architecture one-liner (3 min)

> Multi-tenant, JWT-bound `tenantId`, API meta on every call. Built for enterprise isolation.

## Act 5 — ROI bridge (5 min)

> If we plug in your headcount and incident band, the model shows levers in training time, verification automation, and compliance fire drills — separately from modeled incident reduction.

## Act 6 — Close demo (5 min)

> Next step: technical validation with [IT/security] and a working session on ROI inputs. I’ll send the [engine] one-pagers and the industry brief for [vertical].

## Demo don’ts

- Don’t tour all 12 engines  
- Don’t enable debug outlines  
- Don’t pulse red on everything — critical only  
- Don’t apologize for the industrial look — it’s the product

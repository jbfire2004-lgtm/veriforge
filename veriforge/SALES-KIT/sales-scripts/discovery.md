# Discovery Call Script

**Duration:** 30–45 minutes  
**Goal:** Qualify pain, map engines, earn demo with the right champions.

## Open (2 min)

> Thanks for the time. VeriForge is forged for absolute safety — an industrial platform that unifies training, verification, compliance, and response. I’d like to understand how safety is engineered in your world today — and where it’s still improvised.

## Context (5 min)

1. Sites, headcount, contractor mix?  
2. What tools own training / verification / compliance / incidents today?  
3. Who owns safety outcomes vs IT vs operations?  
4. Any audit or incident in the last 12–24 months that exposed fragmentation?

## Pain probes (15 min)

| Probe | Listen for |
|-------|------------|
| “Walk me through the last expired compliance scramble.” | Compliance Engine |
| “How does a verification get recorded on a busy site?” | Verification / FieldOps |
| “When something critical happens, what screen does leadership open?” | Command Center |
| “Where does risk live — spreadsheet, slide, or system?” | Risk / Predictive |
| “How do contractors prove readiness before gate entry?” | Training / Contractor / Tenant |

## Architecture / trust (5 min)

> We run tenant-aware, with `tenantId` on the wire and in the audit meta. Is multi-entity or multi-business-unit isolation a requirement?

## Close discovery (5 min)

**Summarize in their words** → map 2–3 engines → propose demo agenda.

> Based on what you shared, I’d show [Compliance + Incidents + Command Center] on the forged-metal UI — angular, metallic, critical when it counts. Who else needs to see that?

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Champion + economic buyer identified  
- [ ] Industry brief selected  
- [ ] Demo path chosen (max 3 engines)  
- [ ] ROI inputs requested (headcount, incident cost band)

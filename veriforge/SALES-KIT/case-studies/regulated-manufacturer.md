# Case Study — Regulated Manufacturer

**Label:** Composite narrative · directional KPIs  
**Tagline fit:** Engineered Compliance · Industrial-Grade Verification

## Situation

Discrete manufacturer · 3 plants · ISO-oriented audits · strong production culture, weak safety system cohesion.

## Before / after workflows

| Area | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Training | Shift-dependent, uneven | Angular modules + progress visibility |
| Verification | Supervisor memory | ForgeCheck workflows |
| Compliance | Shared drive chaos | Engineered validity + critical cues |
| Culture | Poster program | Measurable badges/engagement |

## KPI improvements (directional)

| KPI | Direction |
|-----|-----------|
| Training admin hours | ↓ ~4 hrs/worker/year equivalent |
| Manual verification minutes | ↓ ~45% on in-scope checks |
| Audit finding recurrence (doc control) | ↓ |
| Culture participation | ↑ measurable completion |

## Compliance uplift

Audit readiness became continuous — not a two-week scramble. Expiry prevention cut emergency cross-functional meetings.

## Engines

Training · Verification · Compliance · Culture · Equipment

## Close line

> “Angular Workflows. Metallic Precision. Our audits finally looked like our production line — intentional.”

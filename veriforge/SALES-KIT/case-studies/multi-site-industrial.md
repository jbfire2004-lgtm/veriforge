# Case Study — Multi-Site Industrial Operator

**Label:** Composite narrative · directional KPIs  
**Tagline fit:** Forged for Absolute Safety

## Situation

12-site industrial operator · 2,400 workers · heavy contractor mix · safety tools split across LMS, spreadsheets, and a legacy incident form.

## Before

| Workflow | Pain |
|----------|------|
| Training | Completions tracked; competency unverified |
| Incidents | 48-hour average to structured investigation start |
| Leadership | No single critical command surface |
| Compliance | Quarterly fire drills for expired docs |

## After (VeriForge)

| Workflow | Change |
|----------|--------|
| Training → Verification | Modules assigned with forge-check handoff |
| Incidents | Capture → investigate → close on one engine |
| Command Center | Multi-site alerts ack/escalate |
| Compliance | Expiry visibility with critical accent |

## KPI improvements (directional year-1)

| KPI | Before → After |
|-----|----------------|
| Time to investigation start | 48h → ~12h |
| Compliance fire drills / year | 24 → 10 |
| Leadership “swivel” tools for critical view | 5 → 1 |
| Modeled incident cost exposure | −8% (conservative model) |

## Incident reduction

Structured capture and command visibility reduced repeat-category events in the model; pair with Risk + Predictive for forward signal.

## Compliance uplift

Audit prep shifted from archaeology to engineered document validity — fewer expiry surprises.

## Engines

Training · Verification · Compliance · Incidents · Command Center

## Close line

> “We stopped improvising safety across twelve sites. We forged one platform.”

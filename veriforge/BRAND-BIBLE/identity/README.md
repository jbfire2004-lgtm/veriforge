# Brand Identity

## Safety engineered, not improvised

VeriForge exists because safety should be **engineered**, not improvised. Fragmented tools and inconsistent standards fail under industrial load. VeriForge is the platform forged to unify training, verification, compliance, and response with precision.

**Positioning line:** Not just software — a foundation forged for absolute safety.

## Forged-metal aesthetic

The product *looks* like the promise:

| Material metaphor | UI expression |
|-------------------|---------------|
| Iron plate | `#0D0D0D` grounds and panels |
| Steel edge | `#424242` borders, rails, idle chrome |
| Hot forge accent | `#C62828` active / critical / focus |
| Brushed metal | Metallic gradient washes on headers and shells |
| Safety marking | `#FAFAFA` type and icons on dark |

No soft lifestyle SaaS skins. No pastel marketing chrome on product UI.

## Angular geometry

Hard corners. Bevels. Intentional edges. Soft radius is a **defect**, not a preference. Geometry signals durability and discipline — the same philosophy as the workflows.

## Industrial reliability

Identity includes behavior:

- Tenant-aware, auditable systems  
- Stateless services, durable records  
- Critical surfaces that stay clear under pressure  
- Reliability as a brand pillar — Strength · Precision · Reliability  

## Red accent system

Forge red is the **hottest** tool in the kit. Use it for:

- Active navigation underline / border  
- Focus rings (`:focus-visible`)  
- Critical routes and alerts (with text labels)  
- Primary CTA emphasis when intentional  
- Progress fills and accent rails  

Do **not** use forge red for:

- Body copy on iron black (contrast insufficient for small text)  
- Neutral structure (use steel)  
- Sole carrier of meaning (always pair with words / `aria-*`)  
- Decorating every card on a page  

## Pillars

| Pillar | Line |
|--------|------|
| **Strength** | Industrial load-bearing systems. No soft edges. No improvisation. |
| **Precision** | Angular workflows. Measured checks. Engineered outcomes. |
| **Reliability** | Forged-metal clarity across training, verification, and compliance. |

## Emblem & lockup

- Forged **V** — angular, metallic fill, steel stroke, optional red accent corner  
- Wordmark / product name may sit in Orbitron  
- Tagline: **Forged for Absolute Safety**  
- Clear space: ≥ bevelEdge (6px) equivalent padding around mark; prefer 16px+ in layouts  

## Manifesto (short)

1. Safety is not optional — it is forged.  
2. Verification must be engineered with precision.  
3. Compliance must be clear, structured, and reliable.  
4. Workflows must be angular, intentional, and built to last.

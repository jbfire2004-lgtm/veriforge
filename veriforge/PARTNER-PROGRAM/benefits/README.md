# Partner Benefits

Benefits scale by tier. All partners receive forged-metal brand assets and sales enablement baselines.

---

| Benefit | Forge | Alloy | Titanium |
|---------|-------|-------|----------|
| **Revenue share / discount** | Standard band | Mid band | Elite band |
| **Deal registration** | Yes | Priority | Highest priority |
| **Co-marketing** | Logo directory | Webinar + case study | Fund + press + events |
| **Priority support** | Standard | Priority queue | Highest + bridge |

| **Partner success manager** | Pooled | Named shared | **Dedicated PSM** |

| **Certification vouchers** | 1 | 4 | 10 / year |

*Commercial percentages are set in the partner agreement addendum — not published here.*

---

## Revenue share

- Resellers: discount off list or margin on services + license referral  
- Integrators: services retained 100%; license referral fee per agreement  
- ISV/API: revenue share on marketplace / referred ARR when applicable  
- Deal registration protects partner for a defined window (see agreement)

Register deals **before** customer commercial negotiation for protection.

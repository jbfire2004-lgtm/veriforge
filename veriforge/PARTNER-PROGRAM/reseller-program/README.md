# Reseller Program

Commercial motion for VARs and resellers selling VeriForge — with forged-metal co-brand and sales enablement.

---

## Pricing tiers (partner bands)

List pricing lives at `/veriforge/pricing`. Partner bands (illustrative structure — exact % in agreement):

| Band | Typical tier | Use |
|------|--------------|-----|
| **Standard** | Forge | Entry discount / margin |
| **Preferred** | Alloy | Improved margin + deal reg priority |
| **Strategic** | Titanium | Best margin + registration protection + MDF |

### Packaging partners may sell

| Package | Includes (narrative) |
|---------|----------------------|
| Forge Core | Training · Verification · Compliance baseline |
| Forge Operations | + Incidents · FieldOps · Equipment |
| Forge Command | + Risk · Predictive · Twin · Command Center |
| Enterprise | Custom engines + tenancy mode + support tier |

Partners must not invent off-list SKUs without deal desk approval.

### Deal registration

1. Submit account + opportunity in partner portal **before** quote  
2. Protection window per agreement  
3. Conflict → PSM arbitration  
4. Titanium gets highest priority on strategic accounts

## Co-branded materials

### Allowed

- “VeriForge — delivered by [Partner]” on angular title slides  
- Partner logo in steel footer; VeriForge emblem primary top-left  
- Joint one-pagers using metallic headers + red accent lines  
- Case studies with both marks  

### Forbidden

- Recoloring forge red / replacing iron black with partner brand purple  
- Rounded “partner theme” on product screenshots  
- Removing “Forged for Absolute Safety” from master lockups when required  
- Implying Titanium status without certification

## Reseller operating rhythm

1. Register deal  
2. Discovery (script + industry brief)  
3. Demo (≤3 engines, forged-metal UI)  
4. ROI workshop  
5. Proposal (co-branded deck)  
6. Close → PSM for provisioning  
7. Delivery (certified resources)  
8. QBR / expansion engines  

## Compliance

- Ethical selling; no disparagement of safety outcomes as guaranteed  
- ROI labeled directional unless customer-validated  
- Security claims limited to published architecture docs

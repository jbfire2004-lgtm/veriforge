### Visual rules

1. Every sub-brand uses the forged **V** emblem.
2. Secondary accent is stripe / rail / glow only — forge red remains primary CTA.
3. Maintain angular geometry and metallic styling.

## Product lines

- Industrial naming: `Forge{Domain}` + suite codes `VF-XXX-01`
- Angular product logos
- Metallic gradient backgrounds with accent wash

## Campaigns

- Angular hero visuals
- Red metallic CTAs
- Core message: **Forged for Absolute Safety**

## Governance

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| Logo | Forged V only; clear space = V stem height |
| Color | Core palette locked; accents secondary; no soft pastels |
| Typography | Orbitron/Exo 2; ban Inter/Roboto/Arial as display |
| Motion | Heat glow / edge wipe; no bounce or confetti |
